# NutriChef AI

An Indonesian-language nutrition and meal-planning assistant that answers a user's questions about recipes, calories and diets, grounded in a curated nutrition knowledge base.

## Language

**Consultation**:
The ordered exchange of questions and replies between one user and NutriChef AI, from its start until it is reset.
_Avoid_: Chat, conversation, session

**Profile**:
The persona, diet goal and allergies a user chooses for a Consultation. The persona is the voice NutriChef AI answers in; the diet goal and allergies describe the user.
_Avoid_: Settings, preferences

**Turn**:
One user question together with NutriChef AI's reply to it. A Consultation is made only of complete Turns; a question without a reply is not part of it.
_Avoid_: Round, exchange
